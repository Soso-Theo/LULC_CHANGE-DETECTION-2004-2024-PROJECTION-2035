What's inside

Two ways to explore the results

🗺️

Interactive Map

A Leaflet-based map with a time-machine slider animating land cover from 2004 through the 2035 projection, plus toggleable hotspot, change-detection, and driving-factor layers over five basemaps.

📊

Results Dashboard

Animated charts covering classification accuracy, area trends, transition matrices, agricultural conversion hotspots, distance-based conversion patterns, and the 2035 CA-Markov projection.

🌱

Five Land Cover Classes

Built-up Areas, Wetlands & Water Bodies, Forest & Natural Vegetation, Agricultural Land, and Bare Land, classified independently for each reference year using Random Forest.

📈

2035 Projection

A Cellular Automata–Markov simulation, driven by six ranked urban-expansion factors, projects the city's land cover eleven years beyond the last observed reference year.
